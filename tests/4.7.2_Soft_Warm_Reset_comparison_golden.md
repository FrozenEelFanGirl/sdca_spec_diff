### Soft / Warm Reset

When a Device detaches from the bus and then reattaches, there is an associated reset within the interface: Soft Reset in SoundWire **Classic** **[MIPI01],** or Warm Reset in **SWI3S** **[MIPI04].**

> **v1.0:**
> When a Device detaches from the bus and then reattaches, there is an associated reset within the interface: Soft Reset in SoundWire **v1.2** **([MIPI01]),** or Warm Reset in **SoundWire** **I3S** ~~(See~~ ~~[MIPI07]).~~
>
These resets stop the audio streams in the SoundWire Data Ports and also affect a few parts of the SDCA behavior (e.g., UMP state machines for downloading to SDCA memories). Typical Devices might also take implementationdefined actions, e.g., modifying amplifier output to avoid pops and clicks, or overriding the behavior to protect the amplifier.
